## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 643

The interface demonstrates rigorous protocol concepts through consistent dark-mode styling and systematic bar visualizations. Usability remains constrained by heavy cryptographic jargon and dense technical controls across common flows.

- Simplify multi-part balance bar graphics into plain language totals.
- Fix modal backdrop opacity to eliminate background bleeding on overlays.
- Separate everyday payment flows from technical credit and collateral parameters.
- Group command palette and settings options by high-level task domains.
- Provide plain-language summaries for bilateral disputes and proof verification.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 814

xln is a genuinely strong, protocol-true wallet: the sovereignty and account screens separate what is enforceable from what is promised better than most wallets, every agreement is co-signed and provable, and the desk and order book are excellent for traders. But it leaks arcane protocol jargon (frame, co-signed, prepare incoming account, credit room) into consumer-facing surfaces and leans on color-only meaning, which will lose the less-technical half of the ten users and dead-ends first-time cross-network swappers.

- Add a single plain-English line above the Home balance bar ('Green = yours now, purple = promised and could fail') and put text labels on the bar segments instead of color-only meaning — helps Rosa, Lucas, Jamal.
- Add first-encounter tooltips or a plain-language mode for the words frame, co-signed, credit line, promised, and prepare incoming account across Pay/Receipt/Receive — helps Lucas, Rosa, Priya.
- Make 'instantly spendable' a single prominent number on Home and replace 'receive up to X' phrasing — helps Jamal and Priya.
- Fix the cross-network swap so the 'prepare incoming capacity' state offers a one-tap 'Add collateral now' step instead of dead-ending the flow — helps Aisha and Wei.
- Replace 'committed frames' with 'signed by both sides' in Activity/Receipt and rename 'Prepare incoming account' to 'Choose where this payment lands' — helps Elena, Priya, and Noah.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 799

The mobile-dark interface establishes a distinct technical identity with uncompromising architectural transparency around bilateral state machines, signed frames, and enforceable collateral. However, it relentlessly shifts lower-level channel management mechanics, raw hex IDs, and idiosyncratic pixel-scale concepts directly onto end users, creating severe usability hurdles for mainstream and non-technical personas.

- Automate bilateral credit and incoming capacity negotiation behind an invisible, intelligent background policy so users are not blocked by manual sliders and capacity math before swaps or receives.
- Eliminate color-only meaning and terrifying balance discrepancies between Home ($155.00), Sovereignty ($40.00 enforceable), and Account ($90.00 owed) with consistent multi-tier balance headers across all screens.
- Demote developer diagnostic tools (Dollars-per-pixel ruler, lot rounding decimals, raw frame numbers, faucet action strips) into an Advanced / Diagnostics submenu.
- Replace dense legalistic and DAO governance jargon ('lazy entity', 'board takeover', 'collateral tariff', 'Depository may pull') with standard fintech and self-custodial terminology.
- Refactor one-handed mobile ergonomic ergonomics: enlarge tap targets, anchor primary CTAs to bottom safe areas, eliminate modal background text bleed, and widen tightly packed action buttons.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 851

Strong, premium and unusually trustworthy fintech UI: the dual 'yours-whatever-a-hub-does vs promised-by-hubs' framing and the frame/proof-backed receipts are best in class and give Elena, Ingrid and Wei exactly what they need. The weak point is the collateral/credit-capacity screens (cross-network swap and move-to-reserve), which drown non-experts in 0.00/Need/credit-limit/buffer jargon and read as a dead-end rather than a step; fixing those two screens and raising secondary-text contrast would lift every persona.

- Rebuild the collateral/prepare-incoming-capacity screens (05b-swap-cross-bottom, 12-assets-bottom) into one plain-language step with a single primary CTA, moving the 0.00/Need/credit-limit/buffer figures into an expandable 'details' layer — the single biggest blocker for Rosa, Lucas, Aisha, Priya
