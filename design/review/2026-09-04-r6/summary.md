## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 847

The desktop dark suite establishes an exceptionally strong, mathematically rigorous fintech identity with its visceral one-scale bar architecture and high-density Desk view. However, secondary utility views (Manage, Ownership, Lend, Receive) suffer from severe canvas underutilization and empty negative space that break the premium institutional feel established by the primary screens.

- Fix severe desktop layout underutilization on Manage, Ownership, and Receive screens by switching from sparse single-column cards to balanced multi-column grids with active metric summaries.
- Tone down high-glare elements (invert the Receive QR code card into the matte obsidian palette and remove developer testnet Faucets from the primary Assets view).
- Standardize visceral scale representations by adding volume depth backdrops to orderbooks and compact delta strips to the committed payment receipt modal.
- Clean up cluttered horizontal text legends beneath visceral bars (on Home, Account, and Sovereignty) by moving secondary breakdowns into hover tooltips or clean inline badges.
- Promote the outstanding high-density Desk interface (16-desk.png) design language across standard consumer views to eliminate dead whitespace.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 823

xln mobile dark delivers an exceptionally coherent and serious fintech aesthetic with genuine cryptographic transparency and innovative visceral value bars. However, cramped touch layouts, unmasked modal backdrops, and technical jargon currently hold it back from feeling like a polished consumer-ready application.

- Fix background scrim opacity on modal bottom sheets (Pay, Receipt, Activity Detail) by increasing backdrop dimming to 75% black with blur to eliminate background text bleed.
- Redesign the 3x2 tier selection grid in Move (10-move.png) into a compact segmented control or horizontal route stepper so numbers never truncate.
- Add 88px-96px bottom padding across all scrollable views (Home, Account, Activity, Manage, Sovereignty) to prevent content being docked or clipped behind the fixed navigation tab bar.
- Clean up technical developer noise across user-facing screens by hiding zero-allowance labels in Assets, moving Faucets into an advanced developer drawer, and replacing 'Lazy' in Ownership.
- Refine the visceral value bar legends and scale indicators (Home, Settings, Sovereignty) by replacing long paragraph descriptions with structured metric pills and clear tap targets.

### openrouter/x-ai/grok-latest · desktop-dark · 726

Comfort Home, Pay, Account and Sovereignty are close to a calm indigo fintech, but the one-scale bar is broken whenever two surfaces share a window, and Manage/Lending/Ownership/Desk abandon bars for vacant chrome or spreadsheets. Trust copy (frames, proofs, runtime) is ahead of visual hierarchy.

- Enforce one $N/px (Home Settings value) on every money bar including Home USDC vs Hub One, Move tiles, Assets rows, Sovereignty counterparties; overflow fade instead of rescaling.
- Kill duplicate number legends under totals (Home, Sovereignty) and duplicate account bars on Pay (left preview vs right Before/After).
- Rebuild Manage as an account list with bars; don't use a sitemap of tiles on a mostly empty desktop canvas.
- Dead-end states: disable Swap/Offer/Sign until amount > 0; if lending API is missing, don't show a live form.
- Desk must keep the notch-bar language in the position column or it is a second product; four KPI tiles repeating Home math should go.

### openrouter/x-ai/grok-latest · mobile-dark · 670

Mobile dark has a real product spine—hero totals, indigo Pay, bilateral notch bars, frame/proof receipts—but it is not yet a calm fintech surface: legends, chips, hex, faucet CTAs and swap-open spam fight the money. Visceral value is honored on Home/Account/Sovereignty and abandoned on receipts, activity, Move truncation and Lending.

- One receipt component (Pay sheet = Activity sheet): signed amount, frame, proof+copy, Done; drop ms latency.
- Home: delete Secured $0 and the 1px=$100 sentence; one bar caption; no hub chip.
- Move FROM/TO as full-width rows with untruncated amounts and a filled Sign & send; never clip 1,500.00 US…
- Activity: cluster open swaps; bars on sent/received only; stop clipping Accou chips.
- Indigo fill only for money commits (Pay, Sign & send, Done). Faucets, Offer-to-pool, Copy invoice, disabled Swap are outline or hidden. Hard-empty Lending when there is no API.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-4.6v · desktop-dark · 907

The desktop-dark variant of xln wallet demonstrates excellent fintech design with strong adherence to the premium dark theme, clear data visualization, and intuitive navigation. The visceral value principle is well-implemented through consistent bar charts, and trust is established through provable transaction details and clear state indicators. The layout effectively utilizes desktop width with appropriate information hierarchy.

- Increase text size for scale indicators and important explanations across all screens
- Standardize amount display format and truncation rules
- Improve prominence of transaction status indicators
- Simplify technical explanations for non-technical users
- Increase size and prominence of key trust elements like proof hashes and frame numbers

### zai-coding-cn/glm-4.6v · mobile-dark · 895

The mobile dark wallet design demonstrates strong fintech UI principles with excellent hierarchy, premium aesthetics, and clear data presentation. The visceral value bars and trust indicators are particularly well-executed. However, some screens suffer from inconsistent typography sizes and visual hierarchy in secondary content areas.

- Standardize typography sizes across all screens, especially for amounts and secondary information
- Improve visual hierarchy in lists and secondary content sections
- Enhance data legibility for small amounts and detailed information
- Increase contrast and prominence of key interactive elements
- Refine the visceral value bar implementation for better comparability
