## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 628

The interface demonstrates a cohesive, dark fintech aesthetic with mathematically consistent account visualizations. However, high cognitive overhead and dense protocol vocabulary hinder mainstream clarity and ease of use.

- Streamline complex bilateral credit and collateral configurations into simplified automated defaults.
- Separate technical developer tooling and faucets from consumer-facing account workflows.
- Improve responsive desktop breakpoints to prevent cramped tabular data on complex views.
- Elevate critical security and dispute triggers into clear high-priority action elements.
- Standardize transaction status feedback and proof exports across pay, receive, and swap flows.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 866

The xln mobile interface establishes an exceptionally rigorous, mathematically coherent design system with unmatched transparency regarding counterparty risk and verifiable state. However, desktop-grade cryptographic jargon and occasional viewport clipping in bottom sheets hinder fluid mobile retail execution.

- Fix bottom-sheet and fixed CTA layout bugs on mobile viewports (especially in 05b-swap-cross-bottom where CTA text clashes with background controls).
- Streamline the onboarding and payment flow for non-technical users by progressive disclosure: hide raw hashes, tick metrics, and protocol terminology behind expandable advanced inspectors.
- Consolidate and streamline the Receive / Inbound capacity workflow so users can receive payments without navigating manual credit slider adjustments.
- Add direct on-chain dispute and settlement execution buttons directly on the Account and Sovereignty screens rather than only proof bundling.
- Clean up action grids across Account and Manage screens into balanced, thumb-friendly 4-card grids or standard iOS/Android action sheets.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · desktop-dark · 765

A genuinely distinctive, premium wallet whose absolute-scale bars, before/after account math and provable-evidence surfaces set it apart from every crypto UI on the market. It is held back from shipping-grade by a small set of data-integrity bugs — collateral shown as 60 in one place and 0 in another, overprecise float dumps, clipped text and empty pool states — plus two screens (cross-swap, faucet) that bury a simple task under credit-limit sliders.

- Reconcile collateral/covered/owed across 06, 15 and 16 from a single derived value so the absolute-scale bars and the numbers tell one truth.
