## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 674

The desktop interface provides rigorous mathematical accounting and provable settlement clarity across advanced clearing states. However, dense protocol abstractions and subtle bar scaling hinder immediate usability for mainstream workflows.

- Simplify the home balance hierarchy to lead with spendable funds while grouping reserve and promised debt under secondary inspection.
- Separate routine swapping and payment flows from bilateral collateral adjustment controls to prevent checkout paralysis.
- Expose prominent one-click cryptographic export and proof bundle actions on activity details and dispute dashboards.
- Isolate developer tools, testnet faucets, and governance management into an explicit advanced settings boundary.
- Introduce standardized visual milestones and explicit timelines for dispute challenge windows and settlement netting.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 834

The xln dark mobile wallet establishes an extraordinary standard of financial transparency, deterministic verification, and absolute bar scaling that blows away conventional Web3 interfaces. However, mobile interaction bugs—specifically floating primary CTA buttons frequently clipping inputs and scroll containers—alongside pervasive consensus-level jargon currently hamper onboarding for ordinary users.

- Fix floating action button container styling across Pay, Swap, and Move screens by establishing a rigid 96px bottom-safe scroll margin so inputs and disclosures are never occluded.
- Calibrate order book depth bars on Swap screen to reflect genuine absolute/relative volume scales rather than uniform fixed widths.
- Translate raw protocol mechanics into accessible plain-language tooltips across On-chain vs Reserve vs Promised balances, especially demystifying 'Lazy board' and 'Depository may pull'.
- Restructure multi-card selection matrices (notably the 3x2 Move screen and Lend/Borrow flow) into intuitive directional steppers to eliminate touch ambiguity.
- Add one-tap share, CSV/PDF export, and clipboard triggers to all receipt, transaction detail, and evidence bundle views.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 816

This is a genuinely differentiated dark fintech UI — the absolute bar scale, the enforceable-vs-promised split, and the per-frame proof model give it uncommon trust clarity for power users. It is held back from shipping grade by a handful of hard data errors (100 bps shown as 100%, a mis-formatted 47-million USDC figure) and by depth-first jargon plus a desktop-only layout that gate first-timers and mobile users.

- Fix the math error on 13-lend: "100 basis points = 100% per term" → "100 bps = 1.00% per 1-day term" with the annualized equivalent; this single error undermines trust for Marcus, Dmitri, and Tomasz.
- Fix 12-assets data legibility: format "47399024" as "47,399.024 USDC available · 52.001 shortfall" and reconcile it with the "100 USDC" amount; confuses Maya, Anya, and Dmitri.
- Fix 01-home polish: replace "Show 1empty" with "Show 1 more" and restore the truncated "Instant:" label — a cheap consistency/trust win for every persona.
- Add plain-language progressive disclosure for "promised / collateral / credit room" on Home, Pay, Account, and Swap so novices (Maya, Sofia, Fatima, Lena) are not blocked by protocol terms.
- Add an explicit cross-network send flow with a fee breakdown and add a true mobile/compact layout — the current set is desktop-only, leaving Fatima's cross-chain task and Lena's one-handed task unsupported.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · desktop-dark · 783

A coherent, premium-feeling dark wallet with genuinely differentiated trust surfaces (frames, proofs, enforceable vs promised money) that most wallets never show. It is held back by duplicated money-state panels across screens, inconsistent terminology for the same figures, jargon (frame, lazy board, inbound room, capacity), and large empty/dead zones on secondary screens.

- Unify the money-state model: one vocabulary (On-chain/Reserve/Secured/Promised) and one canonical breakdown used identically on Home, Manage, and Sovereignty instead of three reworded duplicates.
- Fix the swap failure path (05): collapse the collateral text wall into one line + disclosure, and make the disabled CTA an actionable 'prepare capacity, then swap' button.
- Make all hashes, frames, and 'committed entries' copyable and clickable (03, 07, 08, 15) so Ravi and Kenji can actually verify and export proof.
- Fill or remove dead space: empty-state copy, positions/history on Lend, actions on Ownership, scroll/pagination on Activity; fix card overflow on Sovereignty and Desk.
- Quarantine vault danger zone in Settings: separate Reveal recovery phrase from Lock/Forget vault with confirmation and red-only destructive styling.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 773

A genuinely differentiated self-custody wallet: the one-absolute-scale bars and the 'enforceable right now' vs 'promised' risk framing are real trust innovations, and the receipt/proof/evidence-bundle layer is excellent. But it is gatekept by jargon and density (Enforceable, Reserve, Promised, bps, Lazy, Entity ID, credit room) with no on-screen glossary, and several primary CTAs clip under the bottom nav, so the strongest design is undermined by adoption friction.

- Add a tappable on-screen legend to every bar and a first-run 'what a bar means' explainer, so the absolute-scale signature is readable without entering Settings (helps Maya, Tomasz, Dmitri).
- Fix bottom-nav safe-area so Pay, Swap, Move, and Lending CTAs are never clipped (helps Lena and overall polish).
