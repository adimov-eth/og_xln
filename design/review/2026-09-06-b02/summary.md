## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 654

The desktop interface provides rich visibility into bilateral credit, cryptographic proofs, and off-chain states. However, dense terminology and technical metrics create unnecessary friction for mainstream users.

- Clarify and simplify credit and collateral terminology across balance views
- Separate technical proof details into progressive disclosure drawers
- Streamline swap and transfer flows by decoupling capacity configuration
- Increase typography scale and contrast for critical financial amounts
- Group administrative and security settings into clearer distinct views

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 864

The mobile-dark interface establishes exceptional cryptographic transparency and rigorous state accounting that far outclasses standard web3 wallets. However, its uncompromising exposure of protocol primitives (pixels-per-dollar, tariffs, bilateral frames, and uncollateralized credit mechanics) creates severe cognitive friction for everyday retail personas while brilliantly serving compliance officers and developers.

- Streamline Home balance hierarchy: replace the persistent pixel-scale educational banner with an expandable balance breakdown card and clarify spendable vs guaranteed balances.
- Simplify incoming payment capacity in Receive flow: raise inbound headroom automatically or present an intuitive 1-tap 'Increase Inbound Capacity' action before showing the QR code.
- Demystify Swap fees and collateral tariffs: replace raw algebraic formula text ('0.1 WETH base + 0 WETH gas + 1 bps') with a clean, itemized fee schedule and dollar conversions.
- Humanize address and proof inspection: add verified address aliasing, middle-truncated checksums, and tap-to-expand receipt verification sheets across activity logs.
- De-escalate Settings and Sovereignty visual anxiety: relocate debug calibration tools ('Dollars per pixel' charts) to an Advanced drawer and explicitly reconcile 'Enforceable right now' with total balance.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 784

This is a disciplined, highly consistent fintech surface — the single-scale green/violet bar language and the per-frame proof/receipt model give it unusual trust and auditability, and it is genuinely strong for a risk-literate or technical user. But it leans too hard on internal jargon (secured, promised, credit room, lane, frame, depository, runtime) and tiny secondary captions, which will lose a first-wallet teenager and strain a 68-year-old, so the fastest wins are reconciling spendable-with-balance, labelling the colour bars and cutting the prose.

- Reconcile 'Spendable now $175.00' with the shown 'Total balance $155.00' on Home and Pay — show spendable as on-chain + reserve + secured so it is never higher than the total (01-home, 02-pay)
- Label the unexplained green/purple/grey mini-bars in Recent and Activity (settled/open/created) so colour is never the only signal (01-home-bottom, 07-activity)
- Cut the long prose banners (home 'two colours, one rule', sovereignty 'green is yours...', account 'every frame here is signed...') into a dismissible '?' with a one-line summary (01-home, 15-sovereignty, 06-account-bottom)
- Fix the misleading controls: static 'What is this for?' rows in Receive, the greyed-but-looks-tappable 'Receive USDC from H1' and dim 'Offer to the pool' — give explicit disabled styling plus a reason (04-receive-bottom, 12-assets-bottom, 13-lend)
- Stage the high-stakes actions ('Reveal recovery phrase', 'Forget vault', 'Dispute this account...') behind a confirm sheet and clearer contextual wording so they cannot be fired accidentally (09-settings-bottom, 06b-dispute)

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 764
