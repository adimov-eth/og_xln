## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 642

The interface demonstrates a consistent, sophisticated dark design language with strong technical primitives. However, specialized protocol terminology and dense multi-layered state models create friction for non-technical users.

- Fix modal backdrop transparency and text overlapping bugs on dispute and sovereignty screens.
- Simplify swap and transfer views by decoupling credit extension into a separate preparatory step.
- Reinforce high-contrast text labels alongside purple and green balance indicators.
- Provide plain-language summaries for enforceable collateral versus hub promises.
- Add explicit dual-signature proof inspection and audit trail views in activity detail.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · mobile-dark · 843

A coherent, premium dark wallet whose colour discipline (green = yours, violet = promised) and receipt/proof surfaces are genuinely ahead of most shipping wallets. It is held back by jargon density (rooms, lanes, capacity, collateral tariffs) and small dense tap targets that will lose Rosa and Lucas even as Wei and Ingrid approve.

- Add a persistent, tap-anywhere colour/bar legend so green-vs-violet and the one-scale rule are decodable without reading paragraphs
- Replace jargon labels (capacity, room, lanes, inbound room) with plain sentences plus the number on Pay, Receive and account screens
- Give human-readable recipients (names/avatars, truncated address, copy) on Pay instead of raw wrapped hashes
- Raise all small action tap targets (BALANCES row, route chips, receipt buttons) to 44px and make primary actions full-width
- Show exact fees everywhere and auto-surface route alternatives when the fee difference is material

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 841

The xln mobile dark interface achieves extraordinary technical depth, bringing formal verification and bilateral credit physics directly to the glass with razor-sharp typography and an uncompromising integrity model. However, it frequently burdens everyday retail personas with raw protocol machinery—such as micro-fractional limits, bilateral credit mathematics, and internal frame semantics—where simple financial confidence is required.

- Replace raw 6-decimal fractions and micro-credit calculations across Swap and Receive with rounded 2-decimal numbers and automated collateral buffers.
- Consolidate the 'Two colours, one rule' narrative into persistent, self-explanatory UI chips ([Guaranteed On-Chain] vs [Hub Promise]) with direct 1-tap rebalancing.
- Separate the emergency 'Dispute' flow from standard configuration tabs into a dedicated fail-safe screen with explicit on-chain gas estimates and frame snapshots.
- Clean up the bottom navigation safe zones and primary CTA button hierarchies, removing secondary actions like 'Add to batch' from default consumer paths.
- Provide an expandable full cryptographic audit viewer for proofs, receipts, and co-signed frames with single-tap export for accounting and compliance.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 813

This is a genuinely protocol-honest, high-trust wallet: it shows enforceability vs promise, co-signed frames, credit lines, collateral and disputes with unusual precision, and the green/violet color rule is a rare example of a clean educational model. But it over-loads every non-trivial action (swap, cross-chain, dispute) with jargon and prerequisite steps, and there are a few correctness/show-stopping issues — a 100 basis-point rate rendered as '100%' and competing headline totals — that would confuse even a careful user.

- Fix the Lending bps label (100 bps = 1.00%, not 100%) and show the annualized rate.
