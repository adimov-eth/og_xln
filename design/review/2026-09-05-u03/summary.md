## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 884

The xln wallet desktop-dark interface is an exceptionally disciplined, audit-grade financial tool that translates esoteric bilateral credit and counterfactual settlement into rigorous, tangible visual physics. While the absolute scale bars and clear separation of enforceable collateral from hub promises establish unmatched institutional trust, dense technical terminology and vast pockets of empty space in secondary screens create friction for mainstream and mobile-first users.

- Bridge the capacity-gating gap in Swap (05) and Assets (12) by replacing passive error notices with an automated 1-click 'Auto-allocate credit/reserve capacity' action.
- Redistribute low-density administrative views (Ownership 14, Lending 13, Manage 11) into a unified console layout to eliminate desolate negative space.
- Tame financial jargon across primary flows: clarify 'At risk / Hub promise' as 'Unsecured credit line' and translate basis points (bps) into annualized APY and dollar costs.
- Soften the receive QR code card background (04) to prevent severe dark-mode eye strain, and add one-click copy affordances to all truncated hex proofs and entity IDs.
- Add direct cryptographic evidence export (signed frame JSON bundle) directly on transaction receipts (03) and activity items (07) to empower institutional DAO treasurers.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 869

xln wallet delivers an exceptional, mathematically rigorous dark-mode mobile interface with unmatched structural honesty around bilateral state, absolute scaling, and on-chain enforceability. Its chief weakness is exposing deep consensus plumbing (basis points, raw frame counts, lazy entities, and hub promises) directly into consumer payment and swap paths.

- Hide advanced bilateral collateral and capacity tuning behind a progressive disclosure toggle on Pay and Swap screens so consumer flows stay under 10 seconds.
- Pin primary transaction CTA buttons ('Pay', 'Sign & send', 'Save evidence bundle') above mobile navigation bar to avoid keyboard and scroll clipping.
- Differentiate balance swatch legends using distinct geometric icons (chain, vault, promise) rather than multiple identical green dots.
- Replace cryptographic jargon ('inbound room', 'lazy board', 'basis points') with clear banking/settlement analogies across all entry cards.
- Consolidate multi-copy actions on Receive into a single universal 'Share invoice' action sheet.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 803

This is a genuinely differentiated, high-trust fintech wallet that leads with a single absolute visual scale and an explicit on-chain vs hub-credit risk split — the strongest aspects are the sovereignty/risk screens and the proof receipts. It ships with a consistent dark system and excellent data legibility on core flows, but stumbles on small jargon ('lane', 'hub promise', 'send up to'), low-contrast decimal values, and a few cryptically dense helper blocks that weaken first-timer comprehension.

- 1. Raise the contrast of decimal/cent values and the small caption/legend text so exact balances and risk colors are legible at a glance (01-home, 06-account).
- 2. Add a persistent one-line glossary for the color system (collateral / at risk / you owe / credit room) on every screen that draws a multi-segment bar, not just the account view.
- 3. Replace jargon labels with plain language: 'Hub promise' → 'Hub credit (at risk)', 'lane' → 'route/token', 'send up to' → 'you can send up to'.
- 4. Consolidate the risk breakdown into the home header so newest users see 'enforceable now vs hub promise' before any action (01-home, 11-manage).
- 5. Make the proof/evidence surfaces uniform and tappable — one consistent proof hash format and a deep link to the full proof on both receipt and activity-detail screens.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · mobile-dark · 822

This is a genuinely premium, coherent self-custodial wallet with a real differentiator — absolute-scale bars, bilateral-signing transparency and an 'enforceable right now' framing that would satisfy security- and DeFi-minded users. It is held back on mobile by jargon leakage (hub/lane/bps/Lazy/Depository-may-pull), low-contrast secondary text, cramped 3-up grids, and the absence of a fiat on-ramp and a cross-network transfer path.

- Add a guided first-run + fiat on-ramp ('Buy/Get money in') so Maya can fund and pay without learning hub/route vocabulary
- Introduce inline tooltips/glossary for every internal term (hub, lane, bps, lot rounding, Lazy, Depository may pull, at risk/in flight) and color-code the $ values to their legend
- Ship a real cross-network transfer view in Pay/Move with a bridge-fee line for Fatima's USDT-across-chains task
- Raise secondary-text contrast to >=4.5:1 and replace 3-up FROM/TO card grids with full-width segmented rows to fix mobile tap targets and truncation
- Consolidate competing CTAs (12-assets-bottom five buttons, 06-account five-tile grid) into one primary action plus a 'more' menu, and gate 'Reveal recovery phrase' behind biometric auth

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · desktop-dark · 785

This is a serious, trust-first wallet with a genuinely novel one-scale bar grammar and excellent proof/dispute surfaces that would win over security and DeFi power users.
