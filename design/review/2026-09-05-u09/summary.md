## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 614

The interface demonstrates a disciplined, mathematically coherent design system tailored for verifiable balance mechanics. However, dense protocol concepts and uneven screen compositions hinder broader mainstream usability.

- Streamline collateral terminology and capacity bars into clear available-to-spend metrics.
- Separate developer testnet tools and faucet buttons from production balance screens.
- Refactor multi-step cross-chain swap capacity checks into an automated flow.
- Balance asymmetric layouts by restructuring wide empty cards across desktop views.
- Elevate evidence export and dispute execution controls on sovereignty screens.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 827

The mobile interface delivers exceptional mathematical rigor, deterministic transparency, and consistent proportional bar scaling that powerfully reinforces custody guarantees. However, persistent layout overlap bugs in swap sheets and high technical friction in capacity management hinder rapid mainstream and mobile-first execution.

- Fix bottom button z-index and container padding collision across Swap and Cross-Swap screens (05-swap, 05b-swap-cross) where primary buttons completely mask collateral sliders.
- Automate or streamline the zero-inbound capacity barrier on Receive/Swap so users aren't stopped by manual credit extensions and tariff calculations.
- Add one-tap Export/Share proof buttons on completed activity details and receipts to serve treasury and invoicing workflows immediately.
- Translate internal terminology ('Depository may pull', 'Lazy entity', 'Dollars per pixel') into standard fintech conventions with contextual educational tooltips.
- Enlarge touch targets and safe-area insets across mobile navigation bars, bottom sheets, and route cards to prevent mis-taps on phones.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 836

The xln desktop-dark set is a genuinely strong, coherent fintech UI whose single-absolute-scale bars make risk and guarantee legible at a glance — a real differentiator. It is held back mainly by the absence of mobile proof, no first-class invoice flow, and a dense technical register that trips first-timers on advanced surfaces.

- Prove one-hand mobile usability — add mobile screenshots and a compact layout with large tap targets (satisfies Lena, lifts the responsive score across the board).
- Add a dedicated invoice flow — create, number, due date, line items, share link, settle → forwardable receipt (satisfies Sofia).
- Surface the first-run plain-language guide on Home so reserve/account/collateral map to 'yours vs promised' before jargon (satisfies Maya and Dmitri).
- Show cross-network fees as a flat dollar amount in addition to bps on Swap/Send (satisfies Fatima, clarifies Anya).
- Promote Dispute to a first-class screen from the Account view, with the challenge window and evidence bundle in one place (satisfies Kenji and Ravi).

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 806

The mobile-dark variant is genuinely strong on data legibility and trust — the one-scale risk bars, the 'green is yours, violet is a promise' model, and receipts with proof/frame numbers are a real differentiator. The weakest point is execution: action bars and toasts overlap content on the Swap and Lending flows, and verbose 'how to read this' copy burdens first-timers and mobile users.

- Fix action-bar/overlay content clipping on Swap (05-swap, 05b-swap-cross, 05b-swap-cross-bottom) and Lending (13-lend-bottom): give footers their own scroll/margin so primary buttons never cover labels or sliders.
- Compress the home 'How to read this' box to 2 lines behind a one-tap 'Got it' and merge the color legend into one persistent row.
- Make proof hashes tappable-to-copy (03, 08) and add a jurisdiction label, so verifiers like Kenji and Ravi can grab full evidence.
- Fix truncated text/tabs: 'Accol' tab in Activity (07), overlapped paragraphs in 05b-swap-cross-bottom and 13-lend-bottom — ensure nothing clips on the narrowest phone.
- Reduce density on power surfaces (12-assets-bottom, 13-lend) by rounding displayed limits and collapsing tariff/math into a 'details' toggle, surfacing only the decisive figure.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · mobile-dark · 811

A genuinely distinctive, trust-forward wallet whose one-scale bar metaphor and explicit 'enforceable now' framing beat most self-custody products on legibility and safety. It is held back by layout bugs on the swap flows (overlapping CTA/toasts), inconsistent numeric precision, and protocol jargon that will lose first-timers and mobile-only users.

- Fix the sticky CTA and toast overlapping collateral chips and fee text on 05-swap, 05b-swap-cross and their bottom variants (add safe-area padding, auto-dismiss toasts).
- Standardize numeric precision: one fee rule (never 0.0000 or 'under 0.01' for the same fact), round 6-7 decimal credits/limits to 2-5 with an expand.
- Add a one-tap scan-to-pay / express pay entry from Home so Lena can complete a checkout in under 10s with one hand.
- Add an invoice / payment-request screen (amount + note + share link already exist on Receive) so Sofia can issue and track a payable invoice with a forwardable receipt.
- Replace protocol jargon ('inbound room', 'lazy entity', 'bps', 'in flight', 'Depository may pull', 'basis points per term') with plain-language labels plus optional detail, and add an onboarding 'get money in' path for Maya.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · desktop-dark · 805

A genuinely differentiated wallet: the single-absolute-scale bar system, before/after account deltas, and proof-forward receipts teach enforceability better than any competitor UI. It is held back by jargon leakage (EntityProvider, collateral tariff, Depository may pull), inconsistent naming for the same secured money, and several layout/truncation defects that clip exactly the trust-critical instructions.

- Standardize the secured-money vocabulary and bar legend across Home/Manage/Account/Sovereignty (one name, labeled segments, matching legend swatches — fix the all-green legend dots on Home)
- Replace dense tariff/protocol prose (swap fees, ownership, allowances) with one-line net numbers and progressive disclosure
- Fix clipping and truncation of trust-critical text: Sovereignty per-counterparty dispute path, Desk CREDIT OUT column, Pay route hex
- Split the cross-network swap into an explicit two-step wizard with an actionable error at the disabled CTA instead of a floating tooltip
- Restructure Settings and Assets: guard destructive vault actions at top, demote faucet-collateral widget, give every disabled button a stated reason

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · mobile-dark · 804

A visually coherent, unusually honest wallet: the one-scale bar system and 'enforceable right now' framing are genuinely differentiated and trustworthy. It is held back by protocol vocabulary leaking into consumer screens (frames, entity id, lanes, capacity), overlapping sticky elements/toasts on the swap flows, and risk-critical controls (credit extension, dispute, forget vault) styled like routine actions.

- Fix z-order/overlap bugs: sticky CTAs and toasts clipping critical numbers on 05-swap, 05b-swap-cross, and sheet scrim on 03/04 — this is the only class of outright brokenness.
- Replace protocol jargon with money language everywhere: 'entity id' → your address, 'frames' → signed batches, 'receive up to' → inbound room (with info), 'lanes' → pools.
- Apply the green=violet=risk color semantics consistently: distinct legend swatches on Home, violet account tiles in Move, amber treatment for risk actions (Extend credit, Dispute, Forget vault) with destructive/confirm styling.
- Move all raw hashes and 'hash of board' internals behind truncation + copy chips + Advanced disclosures; make proof/evidence copyable on every receipt surface.
- Standardize money-layer naming and numbers across screens (Home, Manage, Move, Sovereignty) so 'yours $40 / promised $115' reads identically everywhere, and add safe-area insets so no content hides under tab bars.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · desktop-dark · 767
