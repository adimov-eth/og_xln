## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 868

The xln wallet desktop dark mode delivers an exceptionally rigorous, high-precision financial interface with unmatched transparency into counterparty risk and verifiable state. It triumphs for institutional treasurers, hub operators, and security maximalists, but demands lower cognitive overhead and friendlier onboarding guardrails for non-technical users.

- Surface a prominent 'Dispute Hub / Challenge' modal with automated evidence compilation directly from the Sovereignty and Account screens.
- Add one-click 'Export Accounting Receipt' (PDF/JSON with cryptographic proofs) on Receipt and Activity Detail screens.
- Provide plain-language onboarding tooltips and microcopy clarifying 'Credit vs Collateral vs At Risk' for retail users on Home and Pay.
- Integrate cross-chain route estimation and network badges directly into Swap and Move workflows.
- Relocate testnet faucets and debug tools to a secondary developer drawer to maintain institutional production trust.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 802

A visually consistent, honest self-custody wallet with genuinely excellent trust surfaces (Enforceable right now, Where your money stands, per-frame proof) and best-in-class price/risk data legibility, but it drowns first-timers and skeptics in jargon, micro-text, and alarming 'at risk'/'trust only' framing. Fix decimal precision, reframe risk language, and add a plain-language first-run explainer to turn strong design into broad user satisfaction.

- Format all money to readable precision — '0.04 WETH' not '0.039992000000000000 WETH', commas for large amounts (helps Anya, Maya, Marcus, Fatima)
- Reframe risk wording — replace 'At risk' / 'Trust only' / 'trust only $90' with 'Hub credit' / 'Hub-backed' plus a one-line honest definition (helps Maya, Dmitri, Tomasz)
- Add a plain-language first-run explainer on Home — 'Your $115 is in a Hub account; $0 is on-chain. Hub payments are instant; on-chain is slow' (helps Maya, Sofia, Dmitri)
- Provide a visible Dispute action and challenge countdown per hub account (helps Kenji, Marcus)
- Raise all micro-text to 13–14px — fees (0.000050 USDC), capacity captions ('send up to 215.00'), 'This payment 25.00', and long hashes truncated with copy icons (helps every mobile user)

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 864

xln demonstrates extraordinary cryptographic transparency, state-machine determinism, and mathematical precision with its unified absolute-scale bar system and clear visual distinction between on-chain collateral and hub credit. However, its mobile UX is heavily laden with developer and protocol telemetry (unformatted 64-char hex IDs, raw millisecond latencies, 18-decimal token floats, and unanchored rate cards) that intimidates non-technical users and creates friction during rapid payments.

- Truncate and format all raw hex addresses and 18-decimal numbers across forms (Swap, Receive, Assets) with tap-to-inspect drawers.
- Fix mobile scroll and viewport clipping by pinning primary action CTAs ('Pay', 'Execute Swap', 'Save Evidence') into elevated bottom action bars.
- Humanize verification metrics on receipts and activity details (replace 'in 60832 ms' with '< 1s / 60s' and add clear receipt sharing / export).
- Streamline multi-tier transfer/deposit selection in Move and Receive screens by replacing bulky 6-card grids with intuitive segmented source/dest switches.
- Add proactive 'Collateralize' or 'Request Hub Collateral' action paths right where '$0.00 Enforceable' / 'At risk' warnings are shown so users can remediate exposure.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · desktop-dark · 763

A genuinely differentiated wallet: the one-scale bar system, provenance receipts and the Sovereignty view communicate a real safety model better than any mainstream competitor. It is held back by jargon load (credit line, capacity, lazy board, collateral %) and a few screens that ship broken or empty states (Move pre-error, Assets blocked default, Ownership shell).

- Standardize the money-location vocabulary (on-chain / reserve / at-risk / promised) and put the '$X is a hub promise' caveat directly under the Home hero number
- Fix Move's default broken state (amount 250 vs 0 available) and make WHAT HAPPENS track the actual selection
- Repair Assets: split the right column into tabs and default the collateral slider to a feasible position so 'Receive USDC from H1' is actionable
- Add forward/share to the receipt and copy/view-proof affordances (Sofia, Ravi, Kenji)
