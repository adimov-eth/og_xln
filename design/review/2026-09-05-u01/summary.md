## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 857

The design establishes a masterclass in deterministic dark-mode financial density, replacing crypto hand-waving with honest, mathematically scaled representations of bilateral risk and on-chain collateral. However, it still suffers from technical jargon leakage, occasional layout emptiness on secondary screens, and missing end-to-end receipt and dispute execution controls.

- Add explicit, step-by-step dispute and on-chain challenge buttons to Sovereignty (15) and Account (06) rather than passive status indicators.
- Replace developer-centric jargon ('Lazy entity', 'Depository pull', 'basis points', 'Lines out/in') with intuitive institutional financial terms across all views.
- Implement a complete transaction receipt sharing and proof-verification sheet in Activity (07, 08).
- Connect incoming capacity warnings directly to 1-click credit extension requests on Swap (05) and Receive (04).
- Redistribute wide-screen layouts on Manage (11), Ownership (14), and Activity (07) to eliminate dead voids and display multi-pane inspectors.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 824

The mobile dark UI achieves exceptional cryptographic rigor and consistent visual polish, creating high confidence for protocol specialists. However, it exposes raw engine primitives, empty capacity dead-ends, and unassisted credit concepts that severely block mainstream onboarding and rapid mobile transactions.

- Fix the zero inbound capacity roadblock on Receive and Swap screens with an automated one-tap channel/credit setup flow.
- Replace developer-centric debug elements ('1 px = $10', pixel scale tables, 'Lazy' entity) with intuitive financial terms and clean abstractions.
- Add explicit receipt export, proof hash sharing, and challenge-window triggers to turn theoretical sovereignty into actionable verification.
- Streamline the Move and Onboarding flows by unifying internal reserve rebalancing and external transfers into a guided wizard.
- Provide real-time price estimation and output calculation on Swap instead of showing 0.00 for valid inputs.

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · desktop-dark · 763

A coherent, genuinely premium dark console with an unusual and honest risk story (enforceable vs trust-only) that power users and operators will respect. It is held back by jargon leaks (frames, capacity, px-scale, lazy boards), thin detail/receipt screens, and several near-empty pages that fail the first-timer and mobile user outright.

- Build a real receipt/detail view (08): amount, counterparty, frame hash, signatures, export/forward — unlocks Ravi and Sofia
- Inline the blocked flows: swap needs in-place 'Prepare capacity' plus a quote strip; move needs field-level validation with real caps
- Translate jargon to task language everywhere: capacity, frames signed, depository may pull, lazy board, px scale
- Fix the overlapping/clipped text bugs (15 per-counterparty card, 16 book header) and the contradictory 0.00-selection state in Move
- Fill or delete the dead space: merge Manage into an overview, expand Ownership into actual board/keys management

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · mobile-dark · 715

A consistently styled, trust-oriented wallet with unusually honest framing of credit vs collateral, but key screens (Move, Account, Receive) lead with protocol jargon and show dead-end zero states instead of guiding users to money-in. The order book and Sovereignty screens are the strongest; onboarding and funding paths are the weakest.

- Replace every all-zero dead end (Home, Move, Assets) with a single 'Add money / get test funds' primary action
- Strip protocol jargon from user paths: capacity, inbound room, entity id, frames, lazy entity — rewrite in outcome language with optional detail
- Give account and move screens their actual data hero: per-lane credit/collateral/balances table instead of metadata rows
- Turn red error text into inline fixes: attach action buttons to 'Prepare incoming capacity' and 'Amount exceeds' errors
- Harden security UX on Settings: separate Forget vault from recovery phrase, confirm destructive actions, and label exports with format and purpose
