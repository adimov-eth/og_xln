## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 678

The interface demonstrates a cohesive, dark fintech aesthetic with rigorous bilateral state tracking. However, conceptual density and technical jargon hinder rapid task completion for non-specialist users.

- Clarify safe versus promised balance metrics with higher contrast typography.
- Separate technical developer tools like faucets from standard asset workflows.
- Simplify multi-hop swap and cross-network credit setups into guided flows.
- Surface proof and export verification actions prominently in activity views.
- Improve spacing and readability within the advanced desk trading layout.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 839

The xln mobile-dark interface achieves industry-leading transparency into bilateral credit, enforceable on-chain collateral, and cryptographic runtime state. However, it frequently overloads mainstream users with raw protocol mechanics, mathematical formulas, and dense text where simple visual status indicators and guided flows are needed.

- Replace protocol math formulas (e.g. collateral tariffs, tick sizes, bps) with pre-computed fiat and token figures.
- Reorganize the top of Home and Sovereignty screens to cleanly separate instant spendable cash from counterparty exposure.
- Streamline cross-chain and collateral setup in Swap into a sequential multi-step progress wizard rather than stacked conflicting cards.
- Consolidate repetitive technical diagnostics (Runtime ID, Frame numbers, hex addresses) into expandable inspection drawers.
- Redesign receipt and activity export actions to prioritize one-tap compliance reporting and plain-English proof sharing.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · mobile-dark · 800

A genuinely premium, coherent dark wallet with an unusually honest enforceable-vs-promised colour system and strong data legibility on the home and account screens. It is held back by dense protocol jargon (frames, credit lines, bps, lot rounding, lanes, lazy entities) and a few overloaded flows (swap-cross, dispute, settings) that break the otherwise clean one-handed mobile experience.

- Add plain-language subtitles everywhere (Rosa/Wei/Elena): under every amount say what is 'enforceable now' vs 'promised by a hub', and rename jargon (Frames→Signed records, bps→0.01%, lanes→currencies).
- Lift the enforceable-vs-promised split to the top level on Home, Manage and each Account (Ingrid/Wei/Elena) as a per-hub row: '$40 enforceable now · $115 promised'.
- Reduce density on Swap-cross and Dispute: collapse the collateral slider/tariff text and the long dispute prose into expanders and 3 icon bullets (Priya/Aisha/Lucas).
- Add typed-confirm guards to the destructive actions (Dispute this account, Forget vault, Reveal recovery phrase) and enlarge the primary Done/Confirm action (Rosa/Lucas).
- Fix Activity duplication and chip truncation, and add a minimal 'how it works' one-liner on first Receive (Jamal/Priya): explain routing via a hub and that 'settled' means both sides signed.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · desktop-dark · 781

A genuinely distinctive, premium, trust-forward wallet whose colour-and-bar model and per-frame proof story beat most competitors for power users (Wei, Elena, Noah, Ingrid). It loses novices and breaks its own promises on a handful of layout bugs and jargon walls that make the safety story hard to read at a glance.

- Fix the layout defects that hide or clip information on trust-critical screens: the 05b tooltip overlapping controls, the 15-sovereignty PER COUNTERPARTY text overflow, the 16-desk truncated CREDIT OUT column, and the 07 movement-count mismatch.
- Add progressive disclosure so 'capacity', 'frames', 'bps', 'lot rounding', 'lazy entity' and 'atomic forwarding' never appear on a primary action path; keep one plain-language sentence per concept with a 'more' expand.
- Reconcile the home numbers: show why Spendable ($175) > Total ($155) with an explicit yours-vs-credit formula, and make the green/violet rule readable without the tutorial card and without colour-only cues.
- Turn cross-chain (05b) into a guided stepper that surfaces the 'open a credit line first' prerequisite before the disabled Swap button, so Aisha can complete a hub-mediated chain move.
- Add a people-first Pay/Receive mode (name/QR/contacts) so sending to a person doesn't require understanding entity ids, and promote the uncovered-exposure 'Cover it' action everywhere it appears.
