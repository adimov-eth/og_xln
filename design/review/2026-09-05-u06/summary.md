## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 656

The interface demonstrates rigorous institutional engineering with precise state models and cryptographic clarity across desktop views. It requires substantial simplification of counterparty credit abstractions and secondary tooling before non-technical users can operate smoothly.

- Unify bilateral credit limit configuration into an automated background safeguard rather than exposing raw limit sliders on trade flows.
- Calibrate the universal scale visualizer with clear baseline anchors to reduce confusion between settled funds and counterparty promises.
- Consolidate Move and Account matrices into standard sequential destination selectors with straightforward risk descriptions.
- Structure multi-signature governance and proof verification summaries with scannable tables rather than long instructional paragraphs.
- Standardize dark theme contrast across auxiliary modules like QR codes and dense market books to maintain visual harmony.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 812

The interface delivers exceptional mathematical honesty and protocol determinism, anchored by its strict proportional visual scaling and bilateral state guarantees. However, its mobile UX frequently overwhelms casual and semi-technical users by exposing raw hub mechanics, basis point math, and cryptic failure codes.

- Simplify Inbound/Receive UX: Auto-provision incoming capacity or display plain language warnings when an invoice exceeds hub capacity, removing raw Entity ID barriers.
- Humanize Transaction Errors & Proofs: Replace raw strings like 'system/info' on failed receipts with plain-English diagnostics, resolution paths, and quick retry actions.
- Progressive Disclosure for DeFi & Collateral: Hide collateral sliders, tick math, and basis point interest rates behind an 'Advanced' switch so casual swaps remain as straightforward as Uniswap.
- Elevate Dispute & Enforceability Actions: Provide a 1-tap Dispute flow directly on the Sovereignty and Counterparty screens, pairing the evidence bundle download with a direct testnet/mainnet challenge trigger.
- Standardize Action Button Hierarchy: Clean up cluttered button groups (such as the 3+2 Account grid and Move's triple card layouts) to guarantee clean, one-handed mobile ergonomics.

### openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp · desktop-dark · 802

A disciplined, confident dark UI that solves the hardest problem in the product — it separates enforceable from promised money at one absolute scale and makes counterparty risk readable in seconds, with unusually strong trust signals (signed frames, evidence bundle, request-receipt). The wasted opportunity is reach: no mobile proof, a few sparse/under-designed pages, weak primary-button affordance on action screens, and missing task flows (invoice, cross-network fee breakdown, per-fill slippage) that would unlock four of the ten personas.

- Ship mobile screens and a single-column one-hand flow for Pay/Receive; the multi-column desktop layouts cannot pass Lena's 10-second checkout.
- Make the primary action unambiguous everywhere — Swap, Extend credit, Offer to the pool currently render muted-indigo and read as disabled.
- Elevate the promised-vs-enforceable legend from a 12px strip into a first-class, persistent 14px hero explainer on Home/Desk.
- Add the missing task flows: a real Create-invoice screen (Sofia), a per-leg cross-network fee breakdown (Fatima), and per-fill/slippage history in Swap (Anya).
- Fill the sparse empty states (Ownership 14, Manage 11) with the data users need — evidence-bundle preview on Sovereignty and a net-exposure sum per hub on Account/Desk.

### openrouter/qwen/qwen3.8-flash · desktop-dark · 805

A confident, premium, internally consistent dark system whose one-absolute-scale bar device and bilateral-account transparency are genuinely differentiated and trust-building. It ships strong for power users (Tomasz, Marcus, Kenji, Ravi) but loses beginners and mobile users to jargon, overload on Swap/Assets/Lending, and a missing dispute/onboarding flow.

- De-risk the headline: split 'Yours (green)' from 'Promised (violet)' everywhere and add a plain 'what is safe' line on Home so Maya and Dmitri grasp enforceability instantly
- Collapse Swap/Assets/Lending advanced controls (bps, lot rounding, collateral slider, buffer) behind an 'Advanced' disclosure with a guided default path
- Ship the missing Dispute flow screen with a live challenge-window countdown and an inspectable evidence bundle — Kenji's core task has no destination
- Fix data legibility bugs: clock-only timestamps, the pixel-identical Activity-detail state, the clipped Sovereignty counterparty text, and the unexplained 'Depository may pull' label
- Separate destructive vault actions and add confirmation; strengthen selection states on Move and Lending so the dense screens read as intentional, not broken

### zai-coding-cn/glm-5.3-flash · desktop-dark · 817

A disciplined, coherent dark fintech UI whose green=vulnerable-free / violet=promise semantics and provable receipts genuinely differentiate it; Desk and Pay are close to shipping quality. It is held back by three repeated failures: credit/collateral widgets leaking into unrelated flows (Swap, Assets, Lend), clipped right-edge content on the two most data-dense screens, and internal jargon (lazy entity, lines out·in, depository may pull) presented without translation.
