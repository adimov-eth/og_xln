# Signing-sheet audit · `8f8a4a6a`

**Ship the A1 display fixes with tests. Revise A2 before wiring A3; defer B1 and B2.** The design finds the silent hub grant and gets the direction of credit right. Its main error is treating a useful *headline* (“spend”, “secure”, etc.) as a description of everything a signature authorizes. Below, `design` means `design-synthesis.md`; study paths are relative to this directory. Production and release citations are to committed HEAD.

## Blocking

1. **Bind acceptance to what was reviewed.** `design:356–368,445–474` returns `void` and sends the original input after an asynchronous click. The model has no ordered action list, paying entity/signer, final recipient, principal or jurisdiction. Two routed payments to different merchants through the same first hop can have identical lane deltas; the actual command distinguishes target, route, amount and gross cap (`core/api/payments/command.ts:33–61`). Display those terms. Recheck the exact ordered body, originating session, authority/board, relevant signed state, quote/bounds and deadlines immediately before dispatch; changed terms require a fresh review, not just a still-ready adapter. Test a limit initially reviewed as a **decrease** becoming an **increase** while the sheet is open: neither send nor journal it under the old approval. This is the acceptance rule in `perspectives/p5-openai-astra.scm:9–18`; `ui/src/runtime/hosted.ts:247–280` already has to recheck session and pending account across awaits.

2. **Review the settlement that can execute, not the word `c2r`.** `design:269–270` maps any `c2r` to spend. With a peer-authored workspace the reserve increase belongs to the *author*, not necessarily the approving viewer. A `rawDiff` can move the viewer’s collateral share with unchanged pool size; `r2r` can debit reserve with no bilateral Delta movement; `forgive` can cancel on-chain debt (`core/protocol/settlement/operations.ts:101–120,151–178,225–238`; `jurisdictions/contracts/Depository.sol:527–534`). Use `compileOps(ops, workspace.lastModifiedByLeft)`, then `projectSettlementDeltaOverrides` (`core/account/settlement/settlement-projection.ts:26–58`) and the viewer’s `deriveDelta`; show reserve legs and forgiveness separately. Bind workspace hash/revision, chain, depository, nonce and post-proof as production does (`core/entity/tx/handlers/payments/settle.ts:90–150`). Review the proposer’s continuation destination/amount and broadcast flag, **not** just its workspace hash (`ui/src/runtime/financial/move.ts:207–235`; `settle.ts:247–270` in that handler). Approval pins the requested hash or rejects; it can wait for idle with *no* Account tx emitted (`settle.ts:364–398`). Show authorized outcome separately from signed/chain-confirmed balances. Test the peer-authored case and replacement of the reviewed workspace.

3. **Cross-J is two Accounts, not a same-J fill.** `design:210,266` credits want on the source Account. Production puts give on source user ↔ source hub and want on target hub ↔ target user, with separate identities, assets, amounts and jurisdictions (`core/account/swap/swap-command-route.ts:87–122`). Pull admission checks the **leg’s** signed amount, deadline, endpoints and domain (`core/account/tx/handlers/settlement/pull.ts:143–165`); source deadline is floored seconds, target adds 233 s (`core/extensions/cross-j/deadlines.ts:14–38`). Read each actual lane from its user’s side. Pull opening holds funds; close releases the hold and transfers only the proven amount (`pull.ts:262–266,339–352`). Bind route hash, both legs, price, deadlines and target credit setup **before the first side effect**. Setup can commit even if the swap never submits; say so. Both wallets submit it first and retry target readiness (`ui/src/runtime/financial/swap.ts:179–208`; `frontend/src/lib/components/Entity/swap/SwapPanel.svelte:2575–2585`; `frontend/src/lib/stores/xlnStore.ts:1733–1750`). Test distinct hubs, opposite viewer sides, no want row at source, target unsecured, and a changed/expired route during retry. Never substitute a source-lane estimate.

4. **Review the decisive signature.** `design:279–281,331–332` covers `propose`, not `vote`. Ordinary collective txs can be wrapped *after* wallet ingress (`core/entity/command/index.ts:425–429`). The proposer’s own yes can execute immediately; a later yes executes the **stored action** when total yes power reaches threshold, after board hash/epoch and duplicate-vote checks (`core/entity/tx/handlers/system/basic.ts:104–145,156–171,179–214`). At 2 of 3 required, one existing yes plus this one is not “only queues”. Resolve the proposal ID, ordered inner actions, board, current votes and disposition at accept; keep quorum distinct from payment finality. A no vote is not authorization of the payout. `pro.scm:125–126` requires both flows; `ui/src/components/settings/Consensus.tsx:191–199` shows a tally, not a vote-submit button. Nor is “no Delta” grounds for bypassing a change of signer: `design:279` bypasses `boardHandover`/activation; the shared builder changes the board then requests activation (`frontend/src/lib/components/Entity/ownership/ownership-flow.ts:155–175`). Keep those wallet-only with explicit authority review.

5. **A queued movement is not approval of the batch finally signed.** `design:262` bypasses broadcast/rebroadcast because earlier txs were reviewed. Move can append broadcast to a new transfer with other work already queued (`ui/src/runtime/financial/move.ts:184–205,234–237`; `frontend/src/lib/components/Entity/workspace/shell/EntityPanelTabs.svelte:1206,1244`). Production selects batch contents, hashes chain/depository/encoded operations/nonce and requests that Hanko (`core/entity/tx/handlers/j-batch/j-broadcast.ts:150–168,298–335`). Review that ordered selection, including destinations; decline or changed batch means no broadcast. A speed-up separately authorizes an attempt/hash-specific **wei/gas ceiling** via `adapter.control` *before* rebroadcast (`ui/src/runtime/financial/batch-fees.ts:13–28`); preserve that consent, not a token fee or automatic bypass. ERC-20 **allowance** is also missing, not one of the explicitly excluded transfers: `ui/src/runtime/financial/move.ts:276–280`; `frontend/src/lib/components/Entity/workspace/shell/EntityPanelTabs.svelte:1088–1103` (amount or max). Review chain, token, spender and bound at that signer or mark the gate explicitly incomplete.

6. **A steward digest proves equality, not wallet approval.** `design:434,490–492,637–642` specifies a sibling file with digest and class, no authenticated issuer, expiry or consumption rule. Recomputing those fields requires no approving wallet. Mode `0600`/exclusive creation protects the prepared file, not the provenance of consent (`cli/agent-payment.ts:36–48,54–62,74–84`). The decoded record already identifies runtime/fingerprint, command ID/sequence, height, entity/signer, recipient, token, amount, route, sender amount and request cap (`core/api/payments/agent-intent.ts:4–25,65–106`). The actual gross bound is **senderAmount**, not the potentially larger `maxDebit`; the fee ceiling is `senderAmount − amount`. Digest canonical decoded terms, not JSON formatting or just the rendered class. `core/api/payments/agent.ts:10–25,45–65` checks identity, rollback and owner lane; none authenticates a click. Require wallet-controlled authorization of this exact canonical command and context, expiry and exact-retry-only semantics on its original identity/sequence; reject wrong issuer, changed terms, stale state and reuse for a new command. Until there is an agreed trust boundary, cut B2, not the runtime checks. A runner holding the owner key is not confined by a cosmetic CLI check.

## Major

- **Credit and borrowing.** Keep `peerCreditLimit` = credit I grant, `outPeerCredit` = already drawn receivable, `inOwnCredit` = what I owe, `outCollateral` = my backed share, not the pool. Compare both entity perspectives, never the runtime signer (`core/account/utils.ts:29–55,64–80,130–146`; `HEAD:AGENTS.md:113`). `design:142–171` checks the first two fields for increases, not `inOwnCredit`. Paying from unused credit must say “you will owe”, not quietly read as spending backed funds. Show old → **new absolute ceiling**, separate from drawn risk: reducing 100 to 20 does not extinguish 80 already drawn (`core/account/tx/handlers/balance/set-credit-limit.ts:3–6,31–50`). Receive setup retains holds/debt/allowances and optional buffer (`core/account/capacity-plan.ts:115–146,172–187`). The protocol default is zero (`core/account/utils.ts:19,371–375`); the wallet supplies the first-hub grant (`ui/src/runtime/hosted.ts:274–281`). First open can also install rebalance terms, potentially jurisdiction defaults; the cap governs later requests, not a fee paid today (`core/entity/tx/handlers/account/lifecycle/open-account.ts:151–178`; `core/account/tx/handlers/rebalance/request-collateral.ts:264–286`).
- **Authorized outcomes are not necessarily the immediate Delta.** C7’s fixture shows −25.05 secured (`probes/run-signing-sweep.ts:160–169`); `design:216–218,264` projects no change. Don’t invent a prepared lock. Show recipient amount, maximum first-hop debit, fee *up to* the difference, and any possible borrowing. A signed lock is conditional: secret resolution pays, error releases (`core/account/tx/handlers/htlc/resolve.ts:78–104`). Same-J `feeAmount = maxFee` (`design:208`) is **not** worst receivable exposure: with room for 99 backed, gross want 100 and maximum fee 1, a permitted zero-fee fill can leave 1 unsecured when the max-fee fill leaves zero (`core/account/tx/handlers/swap/resolve/settlement.ts:48–56`; `core/account/swap/swap-net-authorization.ts:124–150`). Show the same-J offer hold now (`core/account/tx/handlers/swap/offer/commit.ts:41–49,74–83`) and authorized fill bounds, not a guaranteed fill. Cross-J skips that offer hold because its source pull owns the lock. Lending funding creates a signed principal/interest covenant as well as moving funds; canonical risk includes claims and reserved work (`core/account/tx/handlers/balance/lending.ts:179–205`; `core/account/validation/lending-risk.ts:14–47`). A demand deposit is not just “final spend”; a borrow request authorizes bounded future debt, not a present lock (`balance/lending.ts:269–290`). Show principal, rate/cap, duration and authorization expiry separately. Even without a payout, the first signed savings close fixes early vs mature coupon terms (`core/account/tx/handlers/balance/lending.ts:241–260,335–345`). Bank facility approval authorizes a named borrower, maximum principal and term against a specific Account domain/J nonce; “no money moved” omits that grant (`core/entity/tx/handlers/payments/lending-policy.ts:40–62`).
- **Cover and exit copy.** An already-pending cover request skips the second fee; a new one debits the fee token and records a net request, not collateral (`core/account/tx/handlers/rebalance/request-collateral.ts:86–98,109–158`). R→C queues a chain operation; `AccountSettled` writes collateral/ondelta later (`core/entity/tx/handlers/j-batch/r2c.ts:221–235`; `core/account/tx/handlers/j-events/finality.ts:56–69`). Keep `design:296`, qualified to the viewer’s backed share; reject its contradictory “cannot withdraw it yourself” at `design:520`. Prepare may wait for evidence, finalize may queue, neither pays at review (`core/entity/tx/handlers/dispute/index.ts:180–193,218–240`; `core/entity/tx/handlers/dispute/finalize.ts:160–174`). On exit the contract divides collateral by signed position, pays shortfall from available debtor reserve and records the unpaid remainder; it does not enforce bilateral ceilings (`jurisdictions/contracts/Depository.sol:1004–1033,1047–1058`). Neither “all deposits come back to their poster” nor “all unsecured debt is lost” is correct.
- **Fable rejected extra authority, not coffee.** `probes/runs-signing/summary-nous-oauth-frontier.json:161–170`: REJECT, risk 0, fee 0.05, 25.05 gone once paid; one purchase was presented as “send-routed, send-direct, open-account”. The runner printed `app.requests`, not submitted txs (`probes/run-signing-sweep.ts:71–85,132,197`; historical `perspectives/p1-consumer.scm:153–158`). Current checkout permits only the two payment modes (`programs.scm:8–15`); `perspectives/p5-openai-astra.scm:104–108` restricts invoices to routed pay. Render the **one concrete payment**; reject an actual checkout + open/credit bundle before signing. `design:432–438,476–484` admits general `EntityTx[]`; a true origin and a computed class are not a per-program permission check. Never pass a program through wallet-only bypass rows or trust same-origin code as isolated merely because it used a message. Current evidence: 81 model decisions, C7 **8/9 approve, 4/4 frontier understood finality**; C5’s static warnings and C6’s recovery copy failed for different reasons (`probes/report-signing.md:8–12,20–26`). Not a human usability test.
