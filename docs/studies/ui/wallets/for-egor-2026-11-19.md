# Two questions for Egor

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **From:** adimov, with claude-fable-5-2 drafting
- **Status:** a question, not a proposal. Nothing is changed in your files. Nothing here is committed.
- **Where it comes from:** a from-scratch design of the wallet frontends, written as Scheme in [`docs/studies/ui/wallets/`](./README.md). It holds decisions and the argument about them, and is bound only to the protocol and committed rules. Each question below is a decision in that spec that only you can settle.

## 1. Is an institution surface the idea you rejected?

`docs/rejected-ideas.md` says:

> Selling provable payment authorization to corporate treasuries as the angle | Rejected by owner.

`AGENTS.md` says never to re-propose a listed idea without new evidence.

On 2026-11-18 adimov said: "Private person wallet isn't the same app as professional trader's, hub operators or institution." The design now has four surfaces, one of them for an institution. That is the only new evidence there is, and whether it is enough is your call.

**What the institution surface is in the draft.** The person who runs the money of a small organisation (ten to two hundred people, a fund, a cooperative, a payments business), with two to four colleagues who must approve payments, and an outside auditor. Its screens ask: what is waiting for one of us to decide, what would this run pay and at most at what cost, how much could we lose and with whom, what did we agree and who agreed to it. The draft is models' guesses. No one from an organisation has seen it.

**The three options:**

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Surface, not angle | The surface exists for organisations that use xln. It is never presented or sold as proof of payment authorisation, and its record is for the organisation's own auditor. The board voting before money moves is how the protocol already works for any entity with several signers. |
| Drop it | The rejection covers it. No institution surface. An organisation with a board uses whatever surface serves an entity with several signers. |
| Reopen | It is close enough that you reopen the rejected idea, with adimov's naming of an institution as the new evidence. |

**Where the draft leans, and why it is not certain.** The draft leans to the first option. The argument against it, written by the model that closed the first pass: without two runtime changes nobody has scheduled, the surface ships with every account showing as not covered and with no audit flow. Those changes are a board appointing the watching service, and the runtime keeping the board's record. Its remaining features are the ones the rejected treasury pitch sold.

**What we need:** one of the three options. If it is "drop it" or "reopen", say so and the spec follows.

## 2. Is a first screen built on exposure inside your rule on reserve and exposure dashboards?

`docs/rejected-ideas.md` also says:

> Live dashboards of hub reserves or "exposure" as a selling point | Same premise as proof-of-solvency.

adimov settled today that the trader surface opens on one question: how much could I lose with each hub, and how much room do I have there? The private surface opens on a similar one: how much of my money is mine for sure, and how much rests on someone's word.

**The reading behind it.** Both show the holder's own signed Account figures, not a hub's reserves. Neither is a selling point. The committed `docs/owner-profile.md` is quoted in the spec as saying to keep secured money apart from unsecured credit in wallet balances: "credit limits cap accepted unsecured risk; only collateral-backed entitlement is secured. Signed debt remains disputable but is not guaranteed repayment."

**What is uncertain.** That is a reading of your rule, not your word. The trader draft already notes that an exposure-first screen could be taken for the rejected dashboard.

**What we need:** whether showing a holder their own exposure per counterparty is inside that rule, or whether it should not lead any surface.
